AudioScribe Support

If you need help with AudioScribe, contact us at support.navrion@gmail.com.

When contacting support, include:

  • App version
  • iOS version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce (if possible)

Privacy, export, and deletion

You can delete your account and notes in the app under Settings → Delete account. For an export of your notes, email support. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Response time

We typically respond within 2 business days.
