Privacy Policy for AudioScribe

Effective date: 30 August 2026

AudioScribe is an iOS app provided by Navrion. On the App Store it is listed as AudioScribe Voice (bundle ID app.audioscribe.ios).

Data we collect

We collect only what we need to run the product:

  • Account. You can sign in with Apple or Google. That gives us an account identifier so we can attach notes to you. We do not require your name. Apple or Google may share an email if you choose that option.
  • Voice notes. A recording you make is uploaded so we can transcribe and rewrite it.
  • Text. The raw transcript, the rewritten note, style, rewrite intensity, spoken/written language, and any vocabulary terms you add.
  • Usage for quotas. How many notes you have and how many processing minutes you have used this month, so Free and Prime limits work.
  • Purchases. Apple and RevenueCat tell us whether your Apple ID has an active Prime entitlement. We do not store your full payment card details.

How we use it

We use this data to transcribe and rewrite your notes, sync your library across launches, enforce Free and Prime limits, restore purchases, and respond if you contact support.

We do not train models on your recordings or notes. We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

How long we keep it

Audio is deleted after processing, and in any case within 24 hours. Transcripts, rewritten notes, and account data stay until you delete the note or delete your account in Settings. Quota counters reset on a monthly cycle.

Third-party services

These processors see only what they need to do their job:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store billing.
  • Google — Google Sign-In when you choose Continue with Google.
  • Supabase — Hosts your account, notes, and temporary audio (project region: Tokyo).
  • OpenAI — Speech-to-text and rewrite. Audio and text are sent only to produce your note.
  • RevenueCat — Confirms whether Prime is active for your Apple ID.

Your choices

You can delete individual notes in the app. You can delete your account and associated notes in Settings → Delete account. If you need an export of your notes, email support.

Children

AudioScribe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
