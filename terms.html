Free and Prime

Free: 10 notes, 3 minutes per take, 30 processing minutes a month.

Prime is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store:

Prime includes 10 minutes per take and 120 processing minutes a month. Notes stay readable if Prime expires; new processing stops until you renew or wait for the Free monthly allowance.
